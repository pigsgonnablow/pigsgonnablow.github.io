Without their giant, the little village drifts on alone.

Driftspan

Working title. A giant guards a tiny village on drifting land.

Tap The Protector to select him, then tap ground or bridge to walk him there. He commands the tiny villagers: drag bridge pieces within his reach and they build them plank by plank, each plank costing 3 wood and 2 stone. If he falls into the sea he is hurt; lose all his health and he is gone. An island is only yours once a temple stands on it, and a temple can only be raised on the island where the villagers live. Drag a piece onto open water so it touches an island you own, or bridges leading off one. Geysers burst up from the sea; a bridge next to one lets workers collect. Planks on a route from home to a geyser stay in repair. Planks off a route start to crack after about a minute and fall after about five, unless you build onto them; tap a cracked plank to mend it for 100 crystal power. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to look around. The game opens with the villagers praying at their obelisk until a Protector rises from the swamp (tap to pray with them). Every geyser starts sealed under stone: stand The Protector beside one and choose Bust open geyser (or press E) and he smashes it free with his spiked club; only then can anyone harvest it. The home geyser is his alone: choose Collect crystal, then carry it back to the village. Each crystal is worth 200 crystal power. Automatons can also be ordered to Mine crystals from any geyser they can reach. Tap the village to build a Swamp Automaton for 400. The villagers bolt it together and three of them crew it; tap it and order it to cut trees or mine rock on land you own. Chopped trees grow back from their stumps; quarried stone is gone for good. Desktop: WASD or the arrow keys walk The Protector, R rotates, 1-6 pick a piece.
